The steps

  1. Start Here: your names, the date of separation, court and case number, attorneys, children. Set once; every tab reads from it.
  2. Timeline: 28 stages from separation to after the decree, each dated from your separation date. Past due and not done turns orange.
  3. Assets: every asset with whose name it is in, the value, and where you propose it goes - to you, to your spouse, sell, undecided.
  4. Debts: the same for every loan and card, so the split is the whole picture, not just the house.
  5. Income and Expenses: the budget now and after, side by side.
  6. Money Moved: every dollar that changed hands since separation, with the receipt reference.
  7. Summary prints the split as it stands on one page for your attorney or mediator.
  8. New: Post-Decree - 30 tasks keyed to the decree date (deeds, accounts, insurance, names) with overdue flags.
  9. New: Inventory - household items room by room, proposed to, contested count.
