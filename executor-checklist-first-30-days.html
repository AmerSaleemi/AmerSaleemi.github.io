Named executor? The first 30 days

Every state's timeline differs - check yours

  1. Order 10-15 certified death certificates through the funeral home
  2. Locate the original will; find out whether there is a trust
  3. Secure the home, vehicles and valuables; forward the mail
  4. Notify Social Security, pensions and life insurers
  5. Keep paying the mortgage, utilities and insurance
  6. Get letters testamentary, then open an estate bank account - never mix funds
  7. Apply for an estate EIN from the IRS
  8. Inventory assets at their date-of-death value
  9. Notify banks, card issuers and creditors; freeze the credit file
  10. Cancel subscriptions; keep the phone number for a while
  11. Start a log: every call, cost and hour spent
  12. Meet the probate attorney and calendar the court deadlines
