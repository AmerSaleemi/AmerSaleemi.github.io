The steps

  1. Start Here: your names, the children, pediatrician, pharmacy, allergies (printed in red on every tab), your notification rule in hours.
  2. Medications: each medication, dose, schedule, prescriber, Rx number, which home holds the supply. The names feed the Dose Log dropdown.
  3. Dose Log: every dose - given, missed, refused, given late - by whom and where. Missed and refused rows shade themselves.
  4. Illness Log: symptoms, fever, who was told and when - against the notification rule in your order.
  5. Appointments: who took the child, when the other parent was told, follow-up needed.
  6. Summary: counts by child, by medication and by month - one page for the pediatrician or the mediator.
  7. New: Child Profile - date of birth and age, blood type, conditions, the care team, insurance, emergency contacts, who may consent.
  8. Allergies & Conditions: severity, the reaction, what to do, who has the medication and the plan. Life-threatening rows turn orange.
  9. Vaccinations: the childhood schedule filled in - type the date given; DUE NOW and OVERDUE flag themselves from the date of birth.
  10. Exchange Handoff: every medication, the last dose given and by whom, the next one due - print it at the exchange or send a photo.
