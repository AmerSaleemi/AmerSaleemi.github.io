The steps

  1. Start Here: the current order, what you are asking to change, target filing date, and a box for your state's legal standard.
  2. Changed Circumstances: what changed since the order, since when, how it affects the child, evidence IDs, witnesses, strength.
  3. Schedule Comparison: the current order, your proposal and what actually happens - overnights per year calculated for each.
  4. Evidence Checklist: the proof for each change - have it, need it, who has it.
  5. Attempts to Resolve: what you tried before filing - requests, mediation, the answers you got.
  6. Costs and Deadlines: filing fees, attorney, evaluator, and every date with days until.
  7. Summary: the case for the modification on one page, ready for your attorney.
