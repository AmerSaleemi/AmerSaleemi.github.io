The steps

  1. Open Start Here and fill the yellow cells once - names, children, dates. Every other tab reads from them.
  2. Dashboard: your share of overnights, missed and late exchanges, incidents, unanswered messages, money owed - the pattern in one sentence.
  3. Parenting Time: every day pre-filled with the scheduled parent. Log only what differs - late, missed, swapped, makeup.
  4. Calendar: 12 months colored by parent from the schedule and your actual entries.
  5. Incident Log and Communication Log: facts, dates, witnesses, evidence - with monthly counts.
  6. Expenses: log each child cost once; the split, your share, the amount owed and what is outstanding calculate.
  7. Court Report: type a From date and a To date and print two pages with every missed exchange and incident itemized.
  8. New: Calls, Requests and Holidays tabs - scheduled calls made or missed, requests allowed or denied, the holiday rotation.
  9. New: Case Contacts, Document Index and Court Prep - the papers, the people and the hearing checklist in one place.
