The steps

  1. For the grown-ups: practice when calm, let kids choose, do it together, and when to get more help.
  2. The poster: "Ways I can calm my body and mind" - twelve things a child can do right now.
  3. 24 coping cards to cut out - move and breathe, think and talk. A child picks one when the big feeling comes.
  4. My coping plan: warning signs, three things I can try, a grown-up who can help - filled in together.
  5. Anger thermometer, worry jar, and "How big is the problem?" - the worksheets for naming the feeling.
  6. Coping skills bingo, kind-words cards and coping cards for school - practice as a game.
  7. Editable in Canva, Google Slides and PowerPoint: type your child's name, rename or reorder any card, add your own pictures.
