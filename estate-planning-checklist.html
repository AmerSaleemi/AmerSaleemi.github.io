Estate planning checklist: what your family has to be able to find

Check the beneficiary forms against the will - the form usually wins

  1. The will - and where the signed original is
  2. Trust documents, if there is a trust
  3. Durable power of attorney
  4. Healthcare directive or living will
  5. Beneficiary forms: life insurance, 401(k), IRA, TOD/POD accounts
  6. Deeds and vehicle titles
  7. A list of accounts and institutions (passwords kept separately)
  8. Debts: mortgage, loans, cards
  9. Insurance policies and the agent's number
  10. Digital accounts and where the password list lives
  11. Funeral wishes and any prepaid plan
  12. Contacts: attorney, CPA, executor, doctor
