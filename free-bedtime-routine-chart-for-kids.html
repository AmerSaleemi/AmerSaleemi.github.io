How to use it

  1. Start the routine at about the same time each night and keep the steps in the same order.
  2. Keep the last steps quiet: story time and a hug work better than screens right before lights out.
  3. If your child lives in two homes, a "good night call" step gives the other parent a regular, predictable spot.
  4. Let your child check the boxes - small jobs give them a sense of control at the end of the day.
  5. Pediatricians recommend a consistent bedtime routine; if sleep problems go on, talk with your child's doctor.
