What to bring to a custody evaluation

Evaluators look for facts, and for the parent who cooperates

  1. The current court order and any pending motions
  2. A one-page timeline of the case
  3. Your proposed parenting schedule, on one page
  4. School records: report cards, attendance, teacher notes
  5. Medical and therapy records for the child
  6. The exchange log and calendar for the last 6-12 months
  7. Examples of your messages - calm, dated, factual
  8. A list of collaterals (teachers, doctors, coaches) with contact details
  9. Photos of the child's room and your home
  10. Written answers to the standard interview questions
