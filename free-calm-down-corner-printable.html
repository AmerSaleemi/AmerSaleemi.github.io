How to use it

  1. Make the corner cozy - a cushion, a blanket, a cuddly toy and the poster at child height.
  2. A calm-down corner is a place to settle, not a punishment. Stay close, keep your voice low.
  3. Practice the steps together when everyone is calm, so they are familiar when a big feeling comes.
  4. Talk about what happened once your child is calm, not during the big feeling.
  5. Pair it with the free breathing exercises and feelings chart on this site.
