How to use it

  1. Try them together when everyone is calm, so they are easy to use when a big feeling comes.
  2. Count slowly out loud: in for 3 or 4, out for 4 or 5.
  3. Let your child pick a favorite and add it to their calm-down corner.
  4. Star and hand breathing give busy hands something to do - trace with a finger while you breathe.
  5. Short and often beats long and rare: two or three breaths is enough to start.
