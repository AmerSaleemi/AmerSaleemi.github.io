Funeral Guest Book Template

The steps

  1. Open the Buyer Guide and click a Canva or Google Slides button (free accounts work), or open the .pptx in PowerPoint or Keynote.
  2. The cover: the photo, the name, the dates, "Guest Book" and the service line. Type over the sample; print it once on cardstock.
  3. Sign-in sheet: name, address or email, "how I knew her" - 15 guests per page. Print as many as you need; it becomes your thank-you list.
  4. Share a memory: three blocks with ruled lines - leave a few on the tables with pens.
  5. New: The service (date, place, officiant, readings, music, pallbearers ...) and A message for the family.
  6. New: Flowers, cards and gifts received, and Donations made in their memory - with the sign-in sheets, your complete thank-you list.
  7. The 5 x 7 table sign: "Please sign our guest book" - print on cardstock and stand it in any frame.
  8. Six designs, 7 pages each. A binder, a report folder or a print shop's spiral binding holds it together.
